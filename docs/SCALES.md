# Complete scales in version 2.0

Open **Acompañante → Material musical**. Select a scale to place its full ascending octave into eight spaces. No audio starts until Listen is pressed. Complete scale restores the full octave after editing. The repeat-arrow choice shows and plays the same eight cards twice.

| Scale | Notes in order | Pitch range | Semitone steps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Do mayor / C major | Do · Re · Mi · Fa · Sol · La · Si · Do | C4 to C5 | 2 · 2 · 1 · 2 · 2 · 2 · 1 |
| La menor natural / A natural minor | La · Si · Do · Re · Mi · Fa · Sol · La | A3 to A4 | 2 · 1 · 2 · 2 · 1 · 2 · 2 |

Each scale includes its closing octave. The synthesizer uses equal temperament with A4 at 440 Hz. C4 is approximately 261.626 Hz, C5 approximately 523.251 Hz, and A3 is 220 Hz. All scale notes share a gentle piano-like timbre so that pitch changes are comparable.

The same pitch uses the same picture in the collection, the sequence, and the loop preview. It also keeps the same picture when switching scales. A downward arrow marks octave 3; an upward arrow marks octave 5. Octave 4 pictures have no arrow. The pictures represent pitches; they do not label feelings.

## Introduce gradually

Demonstrate the complete scale first if she wants to listen. Point to each picture as it plays. Then offer a two-picture sequence using notes from that scale through the sequence-space setting. The eight note pictures remain available, and Complete scale returns to the full eight-note sequence when wanted. Use her familiar picture-card method and stop whenever she chooses.

Switching collections changes the current sequence. Undo recovers the previous sequence in the current session. The default two-instrument activity remains available under Sounds. Version 1 JSON projects can be opened, and version 2 exports include the chosen scale and exact note cards.
